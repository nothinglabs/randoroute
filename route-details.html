Washington Bike Safety

Route details

Plan a route

Use Start and End in the map bar, then tap the map or search for each place. Use + after both are set to add an optional stop.

Choose a routing style

Direct is the quickest ride. Balanced avoids bad roads when the detour is reasonable. Low-stress is willing to detour farther to avoid rule-failing roads.

Understand the map

Tap Bike routes, Highways, Caution, or Fails rules in the route card to highlight those portions of the line. A red pulse marks a road the router could not avoid under your current rules.

Adjust your rules

In Routing Settings, set your shoulder and speed requirements. The app avoids roads that fail them unless there is no reasonable alternative.

Use navigation

After making a route, tap Navigate and allow location access. If you leave the route, the navigation banner offers Reroute back to the nearest planned point.
