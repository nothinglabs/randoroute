Routing weights

Weights have been modified
  • Above 1 avoids, below 1 seeks out. Each value multiplies how long a stretch of road looks to the router.
  • Three sliders = three searches. Every route is searched as Direct, Balanced and Low stress; that's what makes A–F differ. A slider only moves its own mode.
